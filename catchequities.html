What It Does

Five signal engines.
One unified opportunity radar.

CatchEquities runs five parallel AI signal engines — Emotional, Lead-Lag, Narrative, Insider, and Institutional — scoring every signal in real time so you act on conviction, not noise.

Opportunity Radar

A live-updating dashboard that scores and ranks active signals across all monitored assets. Strong, Moderate, and Watchlist tiers keep you focused on what matters most right now.

Lead-Lag Detection

Identifies when a leading asset (e.g. BTC) moves significantly while correlated assets haven't followed yet — surfacing the opportunity window before the market catches up.

Narrative Momentum

Tracks AI-detected narrative intensity across 1,000+ assets — US, Canada, Crypto, and Global. Flags when a narrative is peaking or spilling over to adjacent tickers not yet priced in.

Portfolio Insights

Track your holdings alongside active signals. See P&L, signal alignment per position, and get notified when a monitored asset triggers a new high-confidence opportunity.

Explainable Signals

Every signal card includes a plain-language rationale — what triggered it, which engines fired, and the multi-dimensional score breakdown across Price, Sentiment, Correlation, Narrative, and Insider dimensions.

Secure SaaS Architecture

Built on AWS with enterprise-grade security. Your data and portfolio intelligence are protected with industry best practices from day one.
