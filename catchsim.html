CatchSim exists to make security testing rigorous, repeatable, and insight-driven — before an adversary does it for you.

Core Capabilities

Simulate to validate.
Validate to improve.

CatchSim provides a structured research environment for security teams to test their defenses — without the noise, cost, or unpredictability of third-party red team engagements.

Detection Validation

Run controlled attack simulations against your detection stack. Know definitively whether your SIEM, EDR, and network controls catch what they're supposed to — with evidence, not assumptions.

Gap Analysis

Surface the blind spots your detection tools leave open. CatchSim maps simulation outcomes against your coverage model to identify where adversaries could operate undetected.

Resilience Testing

Test the full incident response cycle — from initial detection to containment. Measure response times, escalation paths, and recovery procedures under realistic conditions.

Risk Quantification

Translate simulation findings into quantified risk metrics. Report on coverage rates, mean time to detect, and security posture changes over time with structured data output.

Continuous Improvement

Security posture degrades as environments change. CatchSim enables repeatable simulation cycles that track improvement — turning point-in-time testing into an ongoing program.

AI-Driven Scenarios

Simulation scenarios informed by real-world threat intelligence and AI-generated attack path modeling. Test against tactics your team hasn't anticipated yet.

Research Focus

What CatchSim is built to answer.

The research driving CatchSim is focused on four fundamental questions every security team needs to answer — but rarely can without dedicated tooling.

  • Do our controls actually detect what they claim to?
  • Where are the gaps adversaries would exploit first?
  • How does our posture change as the environment evolves?
  • Can we quantify and report on risk reduction over time?
The Simulation Cycle

A repeatable process for
continuous security improvement.

01

Define Scope

Select the attack scenarios, threat actors, and MITRE ATT&CK techniques to simulate. Tailor the test to your specific environment and threat model.

02

Run Simulation

Execute controlled attack sequences in your environment. CatchSim orchestrates scenarios without impacting production — safe, repeatable, auditable.

03

Analyze Results

Review detection rates, MTTD, escalation paths, and gaps. See exactly which controls fired, which didn't, and where the failures occurred.

04

Remediate & Retest

Address identified gaps with detection logic, policy changes, or control upgrades — then immediately retest to confirm improvement. Close the loop.
